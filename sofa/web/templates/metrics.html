Callers are pseudonymised; phone numbers, names and known addresses are removed from text. This is a best-effort filter: read a sample before sharing. The two training files hold only labelled turns whose caller heard the recording notice, and audio is exported as a file path only (a voice cannot be anonymised). The notice says calls are recorded to process orders and improve the service; get legal advice on whether that covers using recordings to train models before you train on them.
